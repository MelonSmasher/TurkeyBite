# Tutorial

Seven short modules that take you through the TurkeyBite Console using your own network's data. Each has steps to do in the console and a question or two to check yourself.

**How it works.**

* Keep this page open beside the console. Every **Try it** link opens the right page of the console in one tab, which every link reuses.
* Tick each step off with **Mark this step done** as you finish it. Your progress is kept in this browser, and nothing about it is sent anywhere.
* The questions answer back. Pick an option to see whether it is right and why.

Modules 1 to 6 need only the **viewer** or **analyst** role. Module 7 is for administrators.

<div class="tb-modules" markdown>

1. [**Find your way around**](1-tour.md): the overview, whether events are arriving, the command palette and the menus. *10 minutes.*
2. [**Search every lookup**](2-search.md): Explore, TBQL, time ranges, the field sidebar and saved searches. *15 minutes.*
3. [**Why a site has its category**](3-categories.md): reading an event's evidence, and what *candidate* and *incidental* mean. *10 minutes.*
4. [**Triage a finding**](4-findings.md): the queue, statuses, owners, comments, and teaching a rule an exception. *15 minutes.*
5. [**Tune a rule**](5-rules.md): rule types, backtests, schedules and webhook routing. *15 minutes.*
6. [**People, devices and privacy**](6-people.md): profiles, the person behind a phone, device lookup, privacy mode and the audit log. *10 minutes.*
7. [**For administrators**](7-admin.md): sending findings to SAC, labelling uncategorised domains, and the settings that matter most. *20 minutes.*
